# Code of Conduct

## expected behavior

Participants must communicate professionally, critique ideas rather than
people, respect boundaries, and help keep technical discussion productive.
Disagreement is normal. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexual
attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project
chat spaces, community events, and private communication directly related to
the project.

## enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `PROJECT_CONTACT`. Include relevant links,
dates, and context. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation,
or permanently ban participants. Responses should be proportionate, documented,
and handled with reasonable confidentiality.

Maintainers must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves when they
cannot assess a report impartially.

## attribution

This policy is original to this template and may be adapted by projects using
it. Replace `PROJECT_CONTACT` before publishing the repository.
